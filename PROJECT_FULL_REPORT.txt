1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & END GOAL OF THE PROJECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The goal of Factory LP is to transform production-mix decision-making from
heuristic scheduling into a zero-server, 100% offline-capable, explainable
prescriptive analytics workbench.

Instead of stopping at descriptive summaries or predictive demand forecasts,
the application computes exact optimal daily production plans (x = Chairs /
Product A, y = Tables / Product B), prices scarce manufacturing bottlenecks
via dual shadow prices, exposes theoretical edge cases (alternative optima,
primal degeneracy, integrality gaps), and lets planners stress-test decisions
under parameter uncertainty, counterfactual goals, and multi-period scaling.

Canonical Base Instance:
  Objective    : Maximize Z = 40x + 30y  (INR per planning period)
  Constraint 1 : 2x +  y <= 100          (Machine hours available)
  Constraint 2 :  x + 2y <=  80          (Raw material available, kg)
  Bounds       : x >= 0, y >= 0
  Optimum      : x* = 40 Chairs, y* = 20 Tables, Z* = INR 2,200
  Utilization  : 100% Machine (100/100 h), 100% Material (80/80 kg)
  Shadow Prices: u1 = INR 16.67 (50/3) per Machine-hr
                 u2 = INR 6.67  (20/3) per Material-kg

300-Scenario Dataset Findings (Factory_Optimization_Scenarios.csv):
  - Total Scenarios          : 300 (22 columns)
  - Chairs-only Optimal      : 143 (47.7%)
  - Tables-only Optimal      :  85 (28.3%)
  - Mixed (Both Products)    :  72 (24.0%)
  - Profit Range             : INR 792 to INR 16,905 (Median: INR 3,442.96)
  - Average Utilization      : 83.2% Machine, 85.5% Material
  - Binding Frequency        : Material binds in 198/300 (66.0%),
                               Machine binds in 174/300 (58.0%)
  - Average Shadow Prices    : INR 19.47/kg (Material), INR 13.38/h (Machine)
  - Alternative Optima Cases : 3 scenarios (SCN-009, SCN-194, SCN-213) where
                               multiple distinct mixes yield identical Z*
  - Primal Degeneracy Case   : SCN-295 (three constraints intersect at (0, 53),
                               producing non-unique optimal dual multipliers)

3. COMPLETE SYSTEM & MATHEMATICAL WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Optimization & Data Processing Pipeline
  [User Inputs / Preset / URL Hash / Uploaded CSV]
          |
          v
  [assets/lp.js] Syncs sliders, +/- steppers, numeric boxes, extra constraints
          |      and pushes snapshots onto Undo/Redo stacks (up to 40 states).
          v
  [assets/lp_core.js :: solve2D(c, constraints)]
          |
          +--> Calls Two-Phase Simplex [solveSimplexN] with Bland anti-cycling
          |       * If Infeasible -> Runs Phase-I Minimum Relaxation (IIS) LP
          |         to compute exact minimal Delta-b fixes + "Auto-apply fix".
          |       * If Unbounded  -> Extracts unbounded ray & clips polygon box.
          |       * If Optimal    -> Enumerates extreme vertices CCW, detects
          |         alternative optima edges, extracts duals via reduced costs
          |         and B^T y = c_B, and computes slacks.
          v
  [lp.js :: render() & draw()]
          |  Updates 6 KPIs, animated utilization meters, and redraws #lpChart
          |  with overlays (corners, grad-Z, ILP lattice, MGA zone, robust pt).
          v
  [lab.js :: window.__labRefresh(P, res)]
          |  Recomputes all 5 Analysis Lab tabs synchronously:
          |    Tab 1: Core & Sensitivity (Explain, Tornado, Bisection Ranges,
          |           Parametric Curve, Make-or-Buy ROI, Pin & Exports)
          |    Tab 2: Simplex Tableau & Student Pivot Trainer, Primal-Dual
          |           Complementary Slackness, 2D ILP Branch-and-Bound Tree
          |    Tab 3: MGA Near-Optimal Explorer, Counterfactual Explainer,
          |           Inverse Optimization (synced with chart drag/click)
          |    Tab 4: Bertsimas-Sim Robust LP, Multi-Objective Pareto Frontier,
          |           GBDT Surrogate vs. Exact LP, SPO+ Decision Regret
          |    Tab 5: 4-Product Mix & 3-Month Rolling Inventory Scale-Up LP

3.2 Offline Dataset & Stochastic Workflow (data.html & results.html)
  - On data.html:
      SCEN (300x14) -> Regime Map SVG (cross-filtered by regime & min profit)
                    -> Click any dot -> redirects to index.html?scn=<id>
      Uploaded CSV  -> FileReader -> parseAndSolveCSV() -> runs solve2D across
                       every row -> outputs binding %, mean duals, alt optima,
                       and downloadable resolved_scenarios.csv.
  - On results.html:
      SCEN (300)    -> stochasticMetrics(rows, penaltyRate) using fast inline
                       4-constraint vertex evaluation (~195 ms total in Node)
                    -> Outputs Wait-and-See (WS), Recourse Stochastic Plan (RP),
                       Expected Value Plan (EEV), EVPI = WS - RP, VSS = RP - EEV
                    -> Shadow-Price Histograms solve all 300 rows and group
                       scenarios by dual bracket; clicking any bar lists rows.

4. ALL 20 PRIORITIZED FEATURES IMPLEMENTED (SOURCE & PURPOSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Researched from LP web apps (PHPSimplex, ATOZMath, Mathstools, GeoGebra, Desmos,
ZweigMedia, Cornell gilp, highs-js/glpk.js, Excel Solver), Google Play Store
OR apps (OR Simplex Step-by-Step Solver, Linprog, Simplex Calculator, Linear
Programming), and academic Operations Research / XAI papers:

  #01 Dynamic Constraint Builder (<=, >=, = & Demand Caps)
      File: lp_core.js, lp.js, index.html
      Solves unlimited-demand gap; supports chair/table caps & contract floors.

  #02 2D Integer Linear Programming (ILP) & Branch-and-Bound Tree
      File: lp_core.js (solveILP2D), lab.js, index.html
      Finds exact integer mix, computes rounding cost gap, renders clickable tree.

  #03 Step-by-Step Two-Phase Simplex Tableau & Student Pivot Trainer
      File: lp_core.js (solveSimplexN), lab.js
      Shows Phase I/II iterations, theta ratios, Zj-Cj, and interactive pivot test.

  #04 Modeling to Generate Alternatives (MGA) Explorer [Brill et al., 1982]
      File: lp_core.js (solveMGA), lab.js
      Shades near-optimal region (e.g. >=95% of Z*) and surfaces distinct mixes.

  #05 Counterfactual Optimization Explainer [Kurtz, Birbil & den Hertog, 2024]
      File: lp_core.js (solveCounterfactual), lab.js
      Computes minimal L1 profit shift to flip optimal regime or reach target Z.

  #06 Budgeted Robust Optimization & Price of Robustness [Bertsimas & Sim, 2004]
      File: lp_core.js (solveRobust2D), lab.js
      Linearized robust counterpart with Gamma slider [0..2] & Theorem 2 bound.

  #07 Primal <-> Dual Side-by-Side Visualizer & Complementary Slackness
      File: lp_core.js (buildDual), lab.js
      Auto-generates algebraic Dual LP, plots 2D Dual polygon, verifies s_i*u_i=0.

  #08 Multi-Product (4-Product) & Multi-Period (3-Month Inventory) Scale-Up LP
      File: lp_core.js (solveSimplexN), lab.js, index.html
      Pure-JS n-variable solver supporting Chairs/Tables/Desks/Cabinets and
      10-variable rolling inventory carryover with holding costs.

  #09 Parametric Capacity & Profit Breakpoint Curves
      File: lp_core.js (parametricCurve), lab.js
      Interactive piecewise-linear Z*(v) plot with basis-change breakpoints.

  #10 Capacity "Make-or-Buy" ROI & Budget Optimizer
      File: lp_core.js (solveCapacityROI), lab.js
      Maximizes net profit minus expansion spend under hourly/kg supplier quotes.

  #11 Interactive 300-Scenario Regime Constellation Map
      File: pages.js, data.html
      Cross-filterable 300-point scatter plot with hover HUD & click-to-load.

  #12 Two-Stage Stochastic Analysis (EVPI & VSS) & Penalty Simulator
      File: lp_core.js (stochasticMetrics), pages.js, results.html
      Quantifies value of perfect forecast (EVPI) & stochastic planning (VSS).

  #13 Decision-Focused SPO+ Regret Benchmark [Elmachtoub & Grigas, 2021]
      File: lp_core.js (spoBenchmark), lab.js
      Compares profit forecast error (MAPE) against true decision regret across
      40 noisy trials.

  #14 Inverse Optimization ("Why Was This Plan Chosen?") [Ahuja & Orlin, 2001]
      File: lp_core.js (solveInverse2D), lab.js
      Finds minimum L1 implied profits making any dragged/chosen point optimal.

  #15 Multi-Objective Pareto Frontier (Profit vs. Energy/Scrap)
      File: lp_core.js (solvePareto), lab.js
      Epsilon-constraint non-dominated trade-off curve with click-to-enforce cap.

  #16 Exact Rational Fraction Arithmetic Mode (p/q Toggle)
      File: lp_core.js (toFrac), lp.js, lab.js
      Continued-fraction conversion displaying exact duals (50/3, 20/3) & mixes.

  #17 Infeasibility (IIS Minimum Relaxation) & Unboundedness Diagnostics
      File: lp_core.js (solve2D), lp.js
      Finds smallest RHS adjustments to restore feasibility + one-click fix.

  #18 Synced Numeric Inputs, +/- Steppers, & Scenario Explorer Search
      File: lp.js, index.html
      Exact numeric entry, double-click reset, Undo/Redo, and scenario search.

  #19 Saved Scenario Notebook & Classic OR Presets (localStorage + JSON)
      File: lab.js, index.html
      Persistent browser notebook with JSON export alongside preset instances.

  #20 Client-Side Batch CSV Uploader & Re-Solver
      File: lp_core.js (parseAndSolveCSV), pages.js, data.html
      Offline drag-and-drop CSV solver computing batch duals, binding %, & export.
